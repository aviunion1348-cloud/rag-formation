# Helios Grid — Records Retention Memo (Synthetic)

> **Synthetic corpus.** Invented for this demonstration. This memo deliberately
> contradicts the access control policy on one point so the conflict detector can
> be exercised. It is a fixture, not policy.

## 1. Purpose

This memo records the retention period for access review evidence collected by
the platform administration team.

## 2. Retention

Access review evidence is retained for **5 years** after the review period
closes, in the compliance archive. The memo is dated after the last revision of
the access control policy and has not yet been reconciled with it.

## 3. Review

This memo is reviewed annually by the records management group. Unreconciled
differences with the access control policy are expected to be resolved by the
next audit cycle.
