Vendor Onboarding Requirements

Synthetic corpus. Invented for this demonstration.

1. Security Review

Every vendor that processes customer data completes a security review before contract signature. The review covers access control, encryption at rest and in transit, incident notification, and subprocessors.

A vendor must notify Helios Grid of a confirmed breach within 72 hours of discovery. Notification goes to the security team and the account owner.

2. Data Processing Agreement

A data processing agreement is mandatory when the vendor touches customer content. The agreement must state retention limits, deletion obligations, and the audit rights Helios Grid keeps.

3. Access

Vendor access to production systems is time-boxed to a maximum of 14 days per engagement, requires a named approver, and is logged in the access review register. Shared accounts are prohibited.

4. Offboarding

When an engagement ends, vendor access is revoked within 24 hours and evidence of deletion of copied customer content is requested within 30 days.
